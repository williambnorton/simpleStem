# Click track — beat grid, section click, count-in, MIDI Sync

*Rebuilt 2026-07-07. One dynamically generated whole-song click track,
synchronized to whatever is actually playing, consumed four ways.*

## The beat grid

When a song (or drum pattern, or backing track) loads, the visualizer decodes
the audio, detects onsets, and fits a beat grid to them: the BPM from
metadata is the prior, the period may flex ±1.5% and the phase is chosen so
grid lines land on the audio's real transients. The result is published as
`window.songBeatGrid` and logged:

```
[visualizer] beat grid: 114.4 bpm, phase 0.483s, score 42.3/168 (onset-fit)
```

Because the grid is rebuilt from the audio each time the visualizer source
changes, the click stays true no matter the source — 6 stems, backing track,
or drum machine (whose grid uses the BPM in the pattern's `<bpm>@` filename).
SEMI/FINE changes need no special handling: the grid lives in song-time and
the scheduler converts through the current playback rate.

## Where the click sounds: the OTHER strip

Click audio (triangle blip, 1800 Hz on downbeats, 1200 Hz otherwise) is
injected into the **OTHER channel strip**. Its fader sets the click volume,
its M/S buttons mute/solo it, and its output routing decides where it lands
on the XR18. Want the click only in the drummer's wedge? Route OTHER there.
Want it gone? Pull the OTHER fader or mute the strip.

## The controls (on the OTHER strip header)

The clock and count-in buttons live ON the Other channel strip — clock on
the left of the label, count-in on the right — because the click IS that
strip's audio.

1. **Clock button** — toggles the click track, and records it: each press
   drops a `click track on` / `click track off` ACTION into the song
   (markers `⏱+` / `⏱−` on the yellow lane, deletable like any action,
   persisted via SAVE / song-end auto-save). Turning ON quantizes the
   on-point to the NEXT tracked beat, so the click always enters cleanly
   on the beat; turning OFF is immediate. On replay the click plays
   exactly where you set it. GREEN flashing on the beat = ready to press
   (and visual proof the tracker is locked); YELLOW flashing = click ON at
   the playhead.
2. **Count-in button (hash)** — extends the click track to 4 grid beats
   BEFORE each click-on point, and arms the song-start count-in (4 clicks
   projected back from the first real downbeat). Saved per song.

**MIDI Sync** (beneath the visualizer) — while lit, the portal keeps a
24-ppqn MIDI clock running on every connected MIDI output whenever a song
plays: `start` at play, tempo = grid BPM × playback rate (SEMI-aware,
updates live), `stop` at pause/stop. This is what locks hardware loopers to
playback. The clock is generated by the MIDI sidecar (`midi_sidecar.py`,
brought up by `./performer.sh start`); if the sidecar isn't running you get
one toast and no spam. Sidecar needs `mido`:
`pipx inject demucs mido python-rtmidi`.

## Lyrics placement is a replace operation

Related change, same date: loading lyrics (the button reads "Fetch Lyrics"
when there's nothing left to place) clears every previously placed
lyric-line action from the song. You never place lyrics onto the same song
twice — a fresh load means a fresh placement pass.

## Legacy saves

Sections saved before this rebuild used a `clickIn` flag (4-beat pre-roll).
A first-section `clickIn` still arms the song-start count-in. The
short-lived per-section `+ Click` / `+ Count-in` flags (2026-07-06) were
retired the next day in favor of the click on/off action model above.

## Gotchas

- On stems songs the grid is fitted to the DRUMS STEM's onsets (a guitar
  strum can't drag the phase off the kick), and beat 1 of each measure is
  chosen by where the low-passed drums envelope — the kick — lands.
  Backing tracks and drum-machine sources fit against the whole mix.
- The grid assumes 4/4 for downbeat accents. Odd meters still click every
  beat — only the accent placement is 4/4.
- Clicks are scheduled early by the reported audio output latency so they
  line up with what you HEAR, not with the internal timeline (~23 ms on
  speakers, more via XR18 USB). If your ear says they're still off, trim
  in the DevTools console: `localStorage.setItem('simpleStem.clickTrimMs',
  '15')` pushes clicks 15 ms later (negative = earlier); takes effect
  immediately.
- The scheduler runs on a 60 ms timer with a 0.3 s look-ahead (1.5 s when the
  tab is hidden). A hidden AND silent tab is eventually throttled by Chrome
  to one tick a minute — irrelevant during use (playback makes the page
  audible, which exempts it), but don't expect MIDI clock from a hidden idle
  tab.
- Mid-song count-in beats live on the same grid as everything else, so the
  4 lead-in clicks always resolve exactly onto the section's first beat.
