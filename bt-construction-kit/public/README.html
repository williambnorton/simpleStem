STEP 1You · any machine

Drop a link

Paste a YouTube URL in the portal's Add box, run webloc_drop.sh <url>, or use the Chrome Quick Action. Each writes a .webloc file.

INCOMING_WEBLOC/ Instant
–failed downloads
STEP 2Librarian · webloc_watch.sh

Download and analyze

yt-dlp fetches the audio once and metadata.py finds BPM and key. A video ID already in the library is skipped. A failed fetch stays behind as a .failed file.

STEMS/<slug>/source.wav 1 to 2 min
–waiting to render
STEP 3Drive · render queue

Wait in line

One .json job per song. The next job is the first filename alphabetically, not the oldest drop.

STEM_QUEUE/<slug>.json Behind every job ahead of it
–rendering now
STEP 4Performer · queue_runner.sh

Render six stems

Demucs htdemucs_6s splits vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other. Finished jobs move to _done/, failures to _failed/ with a log.

STEMS/<slug>/*.m4a 10 to 25 min per song
–songs in the catalog
STEP 5Librarian · catalogwatch

Index the song

New stems trigger catalog.py at once, with an hourly pass as backstop. The portal re-copies CATALOG.json within a minute.

CATALOG.json Seconds, worst case 1 h
–songs in the portal
STEP 6You · the portal

Reload the page

The library table loads once per page visit, so press Cmd-R to see a new song. Stems copy to the laptop's own disk hourly, or right away with Flash Cache.

localhost:3000 · ~/.bt-cache/ After a reload

Where is my song?

Follow the fileA .webloc in INCOMING_WEBLOC/ hasn't downloaded yet. A .json in STEM_QUEUE/ is waiting or rendering. A file in _done/ is finished. A file in _failed/ needs a look.
Already in the library?The watcher only catches the same YouTube video twice. A different upload of a song you have becomes a second song, so search STEMS/ before dropping a link.
Renderer stalled?Jobs sit in STEM_QUEUE/ and nothing new lands in _done/. Run ./performer.sh reset on the Performer. The Step 4 strip turns red when this happens.
Status at a glance./performer.sh status on the Performer and ./librarian.sh status on the Librarian list every service, the queue and the current render.

Updated 2026-10-11. Live readings come from the portal's own endpoints (/api/librarian/state, /api/dashboard/state, /api/health, /api/identity) every 10 seconds, so they appear only when this page is opened from a running portal: http://localhost:3000/README.html on the Performer or the Librarian. Performer service lights are read on the Performer itself; Librarian lights come from its heartbeat on Drive.
