🥁 NOTE MAP
Send a drum to a synth as a note. On a drum's line choose the note (named in the current key), its octave and the channel: Bass Drum:C:E:m13 means a bass drum — C on the drum channel, m10 — goes out as E on m13 instead. Lines are kept as steps of the key, so changing Key moves every mapped note at once, even while rows play. Bank rows and the drum wheel both follow the map; the MPL text stays as written. The map is kept between visits.
